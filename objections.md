# Objections

Every objection from a sales call or cold email reply. Append only.

This is the highest signal content source available to you, and the only one
your competitors structurally cannot copy. Reddit gives you anonymous pain from
people who may never buy. This gives you pain from people who were considering
paying you.

## The rule

**An objection heard three times is a post. Heard once, it is a note.**

## What to capture

After every call, add three lines:

- The objection **verbatim**. Their words, not a tidied summary
- What they were actually worried about underneath it
- The moment their tone changed, if it did

Dismissive email replies count. "We already have a dashboard" and "we use Triple
Whale" are positioning material, not rejections.

## Monthly

Cluster by frequency. The top 5 clusters are the next 5 posts. Each one is
pre-validated, because you have heard it five times from qualified people.

## Consent

Announce recording at the top of every call. Two-party consent states and GDPR
both require notice, and it is normal practice.
