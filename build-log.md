# Build Log

Dump raw notes here after work sessions. Messy is fine.

This is the only source of first person stories. If it is not in here, it did not
happen to you, and it does not get written as "I" in a post.

No structure needed. Bullet points, half sentences, whatever survives the end of
a session.

Worth capturing, because it turns into proof posts:

- What broke, and what it looked like before the fix
- Numbers. Hours saved, rows processed, time to first result
- What the client or user said, in their words
- What took longer than expected and why
- What you tried that did not work

Newest entries at the top.
